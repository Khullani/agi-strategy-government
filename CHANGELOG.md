First public release, two-and-a-half weeks after Cohort 1 closed.

## Cohort 1 — April 8 to May 6, 2026

The first run of *AGI Strategy for Leaders in Government.* Five Wednesdays, ninety minutes per session, Zoom (gallery view), Chatham House Rules.

Four participants, one per lever of governance:

- Private equity — capital
- Illinois state legislative chief of staff — statute
- Colorado Digital Service technologist — procurement
- Turkish scholar of international security — geopolitics

The most significant in-flight change: Session 5 was rebuilt over the weekend before delivery, from *"The Action Plan: Moving from Theory to Legislation"* to *"Beyond the Statute: The Six Levers of AI Governance."* The rebuild was driven by participant pushback in Session 4 that legislation was the wrong primary frame. It became the strongest session of the course and reshaped Part V of the playbook.
