# The Backyard Playbook

**A field guide for what to do after BlueDot — running a five-week salon course on a local AI safety problem, and the larger civic portfolio it sits inside.**

By [Khullani M. Abdullahi, JD](https://techne.ai) · Founder, Techné AI · Host, *AI in Chicago*

---

## What this is

In April 2026 I ran a five-week salon called **AGI Strategy for Leaders in Government** on Zoom. I adapted the BlueDot Impact AGI Strategy curriculum for senior practitioners working in and around government — a private equity investor, a state legislative chief of staff, a digital services technologist, and a national-security scholar. Four people, five Wednesdays, Chatham House Rules, gallery view, cold calls, parrhesia, and a real fight in the room every week.

It worked. Not because the curriculum was perfect — I rebuilt the final session from scratch the weekend before delivery — but because the format created a setting that senior people in adjacent fields almost never get. This playbook is how I did it, and what I'd tell you to do if you wanted to try something similar in your own backyard.

It also sits inside a larger argument: teaching a course is one of six things you should be doing post-BlueDot, not the only thing. The rest of this repository lays out the full portfolio.

## Who this is for

- BlueDot alumni who want to convert curriculum into local impact
- Anyone trying to move AI safety conversation forward in a specific jurisdiction, sector, or institution where they already have standing
- People who keep waiting for permission and haven't realized none is coming
